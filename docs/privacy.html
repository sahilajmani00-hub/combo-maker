Privacy Policy

Combo Maker

Last updated September 2026

Combo Maker is a personal tool, built and used by one person, that composes product photos into combo images and uploads them to that same person's own Google Drive. It has no other users and no server of its own — everything runs on the owner's machine.

What it can see in Google Drive

Combo Maker connects to Google Drive using the drive.file scope. This scope is per-file: it only grants access to folders and files that Combo Maker itself creates through the connection. It cannot list, read, or modify anything already in the connected account's Drive, and it never requests broader access.

Can access
Folders and files Combo Maker creates during an upload.
Cannot access
Any pre-existing file, folder, or shared drive in the account.
Stored where
Authorization tokens live in a local config file on the owner's own machine.
Shared with
No one. Nothing is sold, transmitted, or shared with any third party.

Data this tool handles

The only data involved is the product images the owner supplies and the folders it creates to hold them on upload. No account data, analytics, or usage information is collected.

This invite-only dashboard processes product photos in your browser. Photo uploads, combo generation, and downloads do not send your images to our server. Refreshing or closing the page clears your working photos.

The server stores your invited email address, a salted password hash, invitation expiry, and session records to control access. Your password is never stored as plain text. A required login cookie lasts up to seven days; signing out or revoking access invalidates the session.

The hosting provider may retain technical request logs, including IP addresses. We do not add advertising or analytics trackers. Invitation links are private, expire after 24 hours, and can be used once.

This hosted version does not connect to Google Drive, Cloudinary, or AI services. Those integrations belong to the separate local app.

Contact the owner who invited you to request account deletion, revoke access, or ask about privacy.

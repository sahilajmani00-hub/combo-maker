How this works

  1. Prices live in one place. Each product is priced once, here. A SKU cost is the sum of its products plus shipping.
  2. Editing. Type a new price on the left. Every SKU containing that product re-totals immediately and the top bar shows what the change costs you across the range.
  3. Saving. Save prices stores them with this page, so the next person to open it sees your prices. Export CSV gives you a file to paste into the workbook or a marketplace bulk-edit sheet.
  4. SKU names were read by separator. & splits first, then _ and spaces. Spelling variants were merged, so Kanghi and Kangi are one product, and Gldn_Brket, GoldBracket and Golden_Bracket are one product.
  5. Needs mapping lists the SKUs whose names could not be read. Fix those by hand in the workbook.
  6. Another sheet. Open sheet… reads a different cost sheet (.xlsx, .csv or .json) straight in the browser: it finds the SKU column, re-reads every SKU name with the same rules, and re-costs the range against these prices. Your prices are not touched — they belong to the products, not to the sheet. Save .xlsx writes the current sheet back out as a workbook (Products + Cost Sheet).
  7. Copies. Save copy / template downloads a JSON snapshot of this sheet and its current prices. Open copy restores that snapshot on this device without changing shared prices.
